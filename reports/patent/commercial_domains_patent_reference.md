## 1. Core Inventive Concepts (Patentable Abstract Primitives)

1. **Dimensional Invariant Manifold Projection ($\mathcal{M}_{\text{inv}}$):**
   Mapping continuous multi-channel operational telemetry $\mathbf{u}(t)$ and static asset boundary metadata $\mathbf{p}_{\text{asset}}$ into a dimensionless invariant representation via Buckingham $\pi$-similitude and governing conservation laws.
2. **Two-Tier Cascade with Asymmetric Directional Pareto Ranking:**
   A hybrid retrieval engine where Tier 1 applies zero-copy relational SQL pre-filtering to eliminate chronological drift, and Tier 2 applies an asymmetric Directional Pareto ranking that rewards critical kinetic hazard excursions and penalizes under-estimation of severe shocks (breaking symmetric Euclidean/cosine distance degeneracy).
3. **Zero-Copy Antecedent Sequence Pointer Hydration:**
   Restoring non-Markovian memory in hysteretic/threshold-activated media by hydrating multi-hour/multi-day continuous antecedent sensor sequences ($\pm \tau$) around matched candidate apexes.
4. **Distribution-Free Conformal Quantile Envelopes (CQR):**
   Wrapping predictive inferences in statistically certified coverage bounds $[c_{\text{lo}}, c_{\text{hi}}]$ with dynamic interval adaptation driven by hydrograph/telemetry volatility, preventing LLM hallucinations and generating certified engineering envelopes.

## 3. Recommended Patent Claim Structure

When drafting the non-provisional patent application, consider the following hierarchical claim architecture:

### Independent Method Claim (System-Agnostic Core)
1. **A computer-implemented method for diagnostic monitoring and failure mode auditing of a continuous physical system, comprising:**
   - receiving time-series operational telemetry representing dynamic forcing of the physical system and static boundary metadata characterizing physical constraints of the physical system;
   - transforming the operational telemetry and static boundary metadata into a dimensionless invariant manifold vector based on governing physical conservation laws and dimensional similitude;
   - querying an analytical datastore using a multi-tier cascade, wherein a first tier filters candidate historical records using relational bounding constraints, and a second tier ranks the filtered candidates using an asymmetric Directional Pareto objective that penalizes under-estimation of physical shock states;
   - hydrating one or more candidate matches by extracting continuous antecedent time-series sensor sequences across a predetermined historical window ($\pm \tau$) preceding each candidate;
   - computing distribution-free conformal prediction intervals for a predicted physical state using calibrated quantile regression conditioned on telemetry volatility; and
   - injecting the hydrated antecedent sensor sequence and conformal prediction intervals into a prompt context of a foundation language model to generate an unhallucinated failure diagnosis and certified civil/operational engineering envelope.

### Dependent Claims (Domain-Specific Embodiments)
* **Claim 2 (Electrochemical Energy Storage):** The method of claim 1, wherein the physical system is an electrochemical battery cell, the invariant manifold comprises dimensionless Arrhenius reaction parameters and an overpotential ratio, and the hydrated sequence comprises thermal fast-charge duty cycles.
* **Claim 3 (Wind Energy):** The method of claim 1, wherein the physical system is a wind turbine, the invariant manifold comprises Tip-Speed Ratio and aerodynamic thrust coefficients, and the conformal prediction intervals bound structural blade deflection under turbulent wake deficits.
* **Claim 4 (Electrical Grid):** The method of claim 1, wherein the physical system is an electric power grid, the invariant manifold comprises Rate of Change of Frequency (RoCoF) and synthetic inertia deficit, and the predicted state comprises dynamic line rating (DLR) ampacity.
* **Claim 5 (Turbomachinery):** The method of claim 1, wherein the physical system is an aircraft gas turbine engine, the invariant manifold comprises corrected mass flow and compressor pressure ratio, and the predicted state comprises compressor surge margin and remaining useful life (RUL).
* **Claim 6 (Chemical Reactor):** The method of claim 1, wherein the physical system is a continuous chemical reactor, the invariant manifold comprises Damköhler and Semenov numbers, and the predicted state bounds runaway reaction temperature.
